## A complete workflow, not a collection of disconnected tools

**[AssetDesk](https://assetdesk.mk)** is developed by **[PROCESS IN](https://processin.mk)**. AssetDesk is an IT and physical asset management platform by PROCESS IN. It helps organizations register equipment, assign responsibility, track movements, identify items with QR labels, perform inventory checks and preserve documentation throughout an asset's lifecycle.

The platform is designed around practical daily operations: people need to understand the current state, act on the right information, preserve traceability and continue working without unnecessary administrative friction.

## How AssetDesk works

| Step | Operational outcome |
|---|---|
| **01 — Register** | Create an equipment record with an automatic or manually defined inventory code. |
| **02 — Identify** | Connect the asset record to a QR label and scanner-friendly lookup. |
| **03 — Assign** | Document which person, department or location holds the equipment. |
| **04 — Transfer** | Record handovers and movements between users or locations. |
| **05 — Audit** | Compare physical equipment with the digital register. |
| **06 — Maintain or retire** | Preserve service and return history and close the lifecycle responsibly. |

AssetDesk е платформа на PROCESS IN за евиденција и управување со IT и материјално-технички средства. Ги поврзува регистарот на опрема, задолжувањето, преносот, QR идентификацијата, пописот и документацијата низ целиот животен циклус.
